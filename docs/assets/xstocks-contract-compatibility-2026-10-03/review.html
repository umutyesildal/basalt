What needs to change

XC-01 · High · Complete issuer-token compatibility

Keep the current gate. A reviewed replacement must parse every extension, model issuer powers and validate hook accounts across every transfer. Permanent delegate and pause authority create trust constraints a display multiplier cannot solve.

require!(extension_types.is_empty(),
         WhitelistError::MintExtensionNotAllowed);
// Preserve until a complete replacement policy is reviewed.
// Do not admit a mint just because ScaledUiAmount is present.
XC-02 · High · Reconcile source and deployed code

Build reproducible current SBF artifacts, compare deployment bytes and record the exact source revision. Historical successful transactions are not proof of current code.

assert.equal(rebuiltArtifactHash, manifest.artifactHash);
assert.equal(verifiedDeployedArtifactHash, rebuiltArtifactHash);
assert.equal(manifest.sourceCommit, expectedReviewedCommit);
XC-03 · Critical release gate · Replace single-key governance

All three upgrade authorities and the whitelist authority remain one key. Verify the approved governance vault and timelock after a separately authorized migration.

for (const authority of observedAuthorities)
  assert.equal(authority, verifiedGovernanceVault);
assert.equal(verifiedThreshold, 2);
assert.equal(verifiedSignerCount, 3);
assert.ok(verifiedTimelockSeconds >= 172_800);
XC-04 · Medium · Add exact current-revision 3/4-token proof

Use isolated extension-free mocks and a current SBF on a local validator. Exercise creation, mint and redemption with actual raw balance assertions, lookup tables and failure rollback.

for (const n of [3, 4]) {
